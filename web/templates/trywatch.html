See the watching work

The useful part of this only shows itself when the world changes, which you cannot wait around for. So here is a page under our control that you can change yourself.

Three steps

1 Open the examination notice. It says the exam is on {{ date }}.
2 Change the date on that page and publish it. You are now the exam board.
3 Ask here: the exam is on {{ date }}. The agent cites the notice. Then change the date again and watch it withdraw what it told you.

What is real and what is not

Only who moved the date is fake. The citation, the word-for-word check that the quoted line is really on the page, the re-check that notices it has gone, the withdrawal with its reason and timestamp, and the message that arrives unprompted in the thread — all of that is the same code that runs against Stanford and the real exam board.
